Controls & connection help

Move with arrows or WASD. Space jumps. Z, X, or Ctrl fires. Up climbs ladders and activates teleporters. Down climbs down or ducks. Touch buttons support moving, jumping, and firing together.

You can stand on your teammate, ride as they move, and jump from their head. Use “Find partner” in the menu to return to them if you split up.

The host can start exploring before a friend joins. Keep both game tabs visible once connected. A dropped connection pauses the game; the guest can rejoin the same room.

On Android, allow Nearby devices / Local network for your browser if the connection fails. Restricted networks may need a TURN relay. Room codes are convenient invitations, not private passwords.
