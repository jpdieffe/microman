Controls & connection help

Arrows or WASD move. Space jumps. Z, X, or Ctrl fires. Up climbs ladders and activates teleporters. Down climbs down or ducks. Phone buttons support moving, jumping, and shooting together. Landscape gives you more room.

Both players share the original room view. Entering another room brings your partner along. The host runs the original game and sends its picture and sound directly to the guest. Keep both game tabs visible while playing.

On Android, allow Nearby devices / Local network for your browser if connection fails. Restricted networks may need a TURN relay. Room codes are invitations, not private passwords.
